The Unified Shipment ID Lifecycle

How data flows autonomously between Transport, Warehouse, Security, and Insurance without redundant paperwork or manual coordination.

1

Dispatch & e-CMR

Dispatcher creates booking. Google Document AI parses commercial invoice, issues e-CMR, and registers SENT-GEO token with Polish Ministry of Finance.

2

90-Sec Insurance Bind

PZU / Warta API quotes and binds OCP & Cargo policy in <90s. Premium reflects real-time telemetry, driver record, and designated route safety tier.

3

IoT Geofence & Escort

Driver enters corridor. 24/7 SOC activates electronic kingpin lock. If high-value threshold is met, armed escort patrol vehicle is automatically dispatched.

4

Buffer Optimization

If granica.gov.pl live API indicates >12h border delay, system pre-reserves buffer dock at Panattoni/Segro hub, enabling safe drop-and-hook rotation.
